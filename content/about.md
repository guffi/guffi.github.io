Gudfinnur Sveinsson is an entrepreneur based in Amsterdam, working at the intersection of climate technology and energy.

He is currently focused on [direct air capture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Direct_air_capture) and e-fuels — synthetic hydrocarbons made from captured CO₂ and green hydrogen — as part of the global effort to decarbonize hard-to-abate sectors like aviation and shipping.

He is Icelandic, which might explain his preoccupation with geothermal energy, renewable electricity, and long winters.
