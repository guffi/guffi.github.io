This is the first post on this site. It felt right to write something, even briefly, to mark the occasion of putting words on the internet under my own name.

I've been meaning to do this for a while. Writing clarifies thinking. It forces you to take a half-formed idea and commit it to something legible to another mind. That process — the friction of explanation — is often where the real thinking happens.

What you'll find here: essays on energy and climate technology, mostly. Some of it will be speculative, some analytical. All of it will be written to be readable, not just correct.

More soon.
