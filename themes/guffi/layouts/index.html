Gudfinnur↗ is an entrepreneur based in Amsterdam, working at the intersection of climate technology and energy.

He is focused on direct air capture and e-fuels — synthetic hydrocarbons made from captured CO₂ and green hydrogen — as part of the global effort to decarbonize hard-to-abate sectors like aviation and shipping.

In 2023, he co-founded Brineworks↗, which builds electrolyzers for low-cost capture of CO₂ from the air and production of green hydrogen. Its first demo plant opened in Amsterdam in 2026.
