About Gudfinnur Sveinsson

Gudfinnur Sveinsson is an entrepreneur based in Amsterdam, working at the intersection of climate technology and energy.

He is currently focused on direct air capture and e-fuels — synthetic hydrocarbons made from captured CO₂ and green hydrogen — as part of the global effort to decarbonize hard-to-abate sectors like aviation and shipping.

He is Icelandic, which might explain his preoccupation with geothermal energy, renewable electricity, and long winters.

This site is where he publishes long-form writing on energy economics, technology, and ideas. The goal is to think in public — to write things that are worth reading, not just worth clicking.
